# Privacy policy

arc-next does not collect, store off-device, share or sell any personal data.

- **No servers.** arc-next has no backend, account or analytics, and makes no network requests of its own.
- **What stays on your device.** Settings, the address of each pinned tab, and a copy of your pinned tabs and tab groups are kept in the browser's local extension storage so they can be restored. You can remove them by uninstalling the extension.
- **What it reads.** To work, arc-next reads your open tabs, bookmarks and browsing history through Chrome's extension APIs. They are used only on your device, to power the Command Bar and the tab cleanup.
- **Web pages.** A small script runs on web pages to open the Command Bar and to enable picture-in-picture. It does not read, store or transmit page content.
- **Arc import.** A file you choose is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Web search.** When you submit a query in the Command Bar, it is sent to your default search engine like any search typed in the address bar.
